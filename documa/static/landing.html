How 60-Second Zero-Touch Auditing Works

Four deterministic agent execution steps transforming raw invoice PDFs into cleared payouts.

1

Document Intake

Supplier or finance team uploads scanned receipt or PDF invoice to gs://documa-receipts-bucket.

2

Multimodal Extraction

VisionAgent extracts itemized line items, unit prices, sub-totals, and vendor signatures via Gemini 3.5 Flash.

3

Contract Audit

AuditorAgent cross-references extracted line items against active Purchase Orders in Firestore.

4

Discrepancy Resolution

DiscrepancyAgent auto-approves compliant payouts or generates formal Markdown vendor dispute notices.
